# API and data-source shortlist

This is a source-discovery guide, not a claim that any provider is currently connected. Confirm current API terms, attribution, request quotas, retention rights, and production-use permission before implementing an adapter. Public web dashboards do not necessarily expose an API.

| Domain | Sweden | Other Nordic countries | Europe-wide options | Access and important limitations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weather | [SMHI Open Data](https://opendata.smhi.se/) for observations, forecasts, and warnings. | [MET Norway API](https://api.met.no/doc/) for Norway and broad Nordic forecasts; national meteorological agencies remain authoritative for Finland, Denmark, and Iceland. | [ECMWF Open Data](https://www.ecmwf.int/en/forecasts/datasets/open-data) for model output. | SMHI and MET Norway offer open/free data subject to terms and identification/usage requirements. ECMWF has dataset-specific terms. Use national warning feeds for official alerts; forecast grids are not warning authority. |
| Financial information | [Riksbank statistics/API](https://www.riksbank.se/en-gb/statistics/interest-rates-and-exchange-rates/search-interest-rates-and-exchange-rates/) and [SCB statistics](https://www.scb.se/en/). | National statistical/central-bank sources; Nord Pool for electricity markets. | [ECB SDMX API](https://data.ecb.europa.eu/help/api/overview), [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/main/data/database). | Official statistical series and central-bank data are generally open, but mostly not live market prices. Intraday securities/order-book data from Nasdaq Nordic and similar venues is typically licensed/paid. |
| Road traffic | [Trafikverket](https://bransch.trafikverket.se/tjanster/data-kartor-och-geodatatjanster/) and the [Trafficdata National Access Point](https://www.trafficdata.se/). | [Fintraffic Digitraffic](https://www.digitraffic.fi/en/road-traffic/) (Finland, open REST/JSON); Norway's [Statens vegvesen](https://www.vegvesen.no/en/traffic-information/) and Denmark's [Vejdirektoratet](https://trafikinfo.dk/) publish national traffic information; [Iceland Road and Coastal Administration](https://umferdin.is/) for Iceland. | National Access Points under the EU Intelligent Transport Systems framework; integrate country feeds individually. | Trafikverket access to selected APIs requires an API key/account. Digitraffic is documented as open. National feeds vary in schemas, terms, and update latency; there is no complete, harmonized live European incident feed. |
| Train traffic | [Trafikverket traffic information/API](https://www.trafikverket.se/resa-och-trafik/trafikinformation/) for Swedish train movements, stations, and disruptions. | [Entur developer portal](https://developer.entur.org/) (Norway, open); Finnish [Digitraffic rail](https://www.digitraffic.fi/en/railway-traffic/); Denmark's [Rejseplanen](https://www.rejseplanen.dk/) provides journey/planned service data; Iceland has no comparable national passenger rail network. | Connect national rail feeds and EU National Access Points; no single public API gives a complete Europe-wide live operational picture. | Trafikverket requires an API key. Entur is open/free under its developer terms. Timetables and passenger disruptions do not expose railway-control telemetry, freight capacity, or full operator capacity. |
| Air traffic | [LFV](https://www.lfv.se/en/) and [Swedavia](https://www.swedavia.com/) publish airport/aviation information; airport/operator sources are better for local disruption notices. | National aviation and airport operators for Norway, Denmark, Finland, and Iceland. | [EUROCONTROL](https://www.eurocontrol.int/) for network-level European traffic/flow information; [OpenSky Network API](https://openskynetwork.github.io/opensky-api/rest.html) for aircraft state-vector research/observation data. | Public EUROCONTROL reports are available; detailed operational systems/data may be restricted. OpenSky has rate limits, coverage gaps, and use-specific terms. Full official live flight/ATC status is not an unrestricted public feed; ADS-B aggregators are commercial alternatives. |
| Maritime / boat traffic | Swedish Maritime Administration notices and national maritime sources; [HELCOM data services](https://helcom.fi/baltic-sea-trends/data/) for Baltic geospatial/environmental context. | National maritime administrations and port authorities. | [EMSA SafeSeaNet](https://www.emsa.europa.eu/ssn-main.html) for EU, Norway, and Iceland maritime-authority exchange; commercial [Kpler maritime data](https://www.kpler.com/product/maritime/data-services) for broader AIS products. | SafeSeaNet is restricted to authorized users, not an open public API. Public live AIS is fragmented and generally commercial. HELCOM is not a substitute for live vessel tracking. |
| Electricity grid status | [Svenska kraftnät control room](https://www.svk.se/en/national-grid/the-control-room/) and selected system data. | [Fingrid](https://www.fingrid.fi/en/electricity-market-information/) (Finland), [Statnett](https://www.statnett.no/en/for-stakeholders-in-the-power-industry/data-from-the-power-system/) (Norway), Energinet (Denmark), and Landsnet (Iceland). | [ENTSO-E Transparency Platform](https://transparency.entsoe.eu/) for generation, load, flows, balancing, and market data. | ENTSO-E's API requires registration and a security token; selected data is public subject to terms. National TSO data is preferable for local context. Some published flow measurements are delayed, and public feeds do not expose all grid-control telemetry or security-sensitive capacity. |
| Oil, gas, and storage | [Swedish Energy Agency statistics](https://www.energimyndigheten.se/en/facts-and-figures/statistics/) for periodic national data. | National energy authorities and TSO sources; [GIE AGSI](https://agsi.gie.eu/) for gas storage and [ALSI](https://alsi.gie.eu/) for LNG across participating European facilities. | [ENTSOG Transparency Platform](https://transparency.entsog.eu/) for gas transmission; [JODI](https://www.jodidata.org/) for national oil/gas supply and stocks. | Public portal views exist; bulk/API access may require registration, API keys, or acceptance of provider terms. JODI and official national stock statistics are periodic and lagged. Real-time oil inventories and strategic-reserve status are generally not public. |
| Datacenters: inventory, capacity, service status | Facility operators and cloud-provider status pages for individually named services; no public comprehensive Swedish inventory/capacity feed. | Operator status pages and national digital/energy authorities; similarly fragmented. | [EUDCA](https://www.eudca.org/) for periodic European industry reports; provider status pages for service-specific incidents. | EUDCA materials are sector context, not a live facility API. Facility directories and detailed market data may be paid/subscription. Real-time utilization, spare capacity, power headroom, physical incidents, and comprehensive facility outages are not publicly exposed. |
| Telecom coverage, capacity, outages | [PTS Bredbandskartan](https://bredbandskartan.pts.se/) for Swedish fixed/mobile coverage discovery; PTS/operator reports for service incidents. | National regulators and operators in Norway (Nkom), Denmark (Digitaliseringsstyrelsen), Finland (Traficom), and Iceland (Fjarskiptastofa). | [BEREC](https://www.berec.europa.eu/en) for regulator coordination/reports; [GSMA coverage data](https://www.gsma.com/coverage/) for subscription/bespoke operator-submitted coverage. | Regulator maps/statistics are generally public but updated periodically. Operator incident pages vary in format. Comparable pan-European real-time capacity, coverage, or outage feeds are not public; network capacity is usually restricted. |
| Data networks / Internet | [RIPE Atlas](https://atlas.ripe.net/docs/apis/rest-api-reference/) for independent reachability and connectivity measurements from Sweden and other RIPE regions. | RIPE Atlas vantage points plus national regulator/operator incident notices. | [RIPE Atlas](https://www.ripe.net/analyse/internet-measurements/ripe-atlas/) and [Cloudflare Radar](https://radar.cloudflare.com/) for network measurements, routing, DNS, traffic trends, and outage signals. | RIPE's collected measurements are largely public; custom measurements use a credits system. Radar API access and limits vary by endpoint. Neither provides definitive nationwide uptime or complete ISP capacity: measurements are vantage-point evidence, not an authoritative outage declaration. |

## Suggested integration order

1. Begin with open, well-documented feeds: SMHI, MET Norway, Digitraffic, Entur, RIPE Atlas, ECB/Eurostat, and selected national TSO information.
2. Apply for API accounts/tokens where access is free but authenticated: Trafikverket and ENTSO-E are examples. Store credentials only in backend secret storage/environment configuration.
3. Add national sources for the five Nordic countries and retain source-specific freshness metadata. Prefer the national authority for local warnings and operational status.
4. Treat restricted, commercial, or non-public data (SafeSeaNet, exchange market feeds, detailed datacenter/telecom capacity, some aviation/AIS data) as optional integrations requiring explicit authorization or paid contracts.

Every observation should retain its provider, geographic scope, observation time, retrieval time, update cadence, data class (live, forecast, schedule, or delayed statistic), and quality/confidence. Missing or stale data must appear as unknown/stale, never as operational.
